What outsiders see

The registry board is public so anyone can audit it: its viewing key is published. This is everything anyone can learn from it.

登記所seen from the street

見えるAnyone with the board key reads

  1. 見That an invoice was registered, and whenVisibleA register entry appears on the board in a block
  2. 見Whether the buyer brought a payment historyVisibleThe entry kind: plain register, or register with a history tag
  3. 見That an invoice was settled, on time or lateVisibleA settled entry appears, with its on-time flag and two signatures
  4. 見That one N appears twiceVisibleWithout the invoice nobody can tell a double financing from griefing

見えないStays with the parties

  1. 秘Who the supplier, buyer and financier areHiddenShielded payments; per-invoice keys; each financier behind a commitment whose salt is new for every invoice
  2. 秘Invoice amount, dates, termsHiddenOnly inside the signed invoice the parties hold
  3. 秘Which invoice an entry is aboutHiddenN is a hash of the whole invoice, including a per-invoice key; it cannot be guessed
  4. 選Discount class and tenor class of each registrationHidden by defaultEntries carry 0/0 unless the supplier opts in to the public index (D-42). An opted-in entry's codes can be read by anyone holding the invoice; the index shows a class only from 5 invoices
  5. 秘Which registration a settlement closesHiddenThe settled tag is a hash of N. Whoever holds N (the financiers who saw the invoice, a fund member, a lender shown the buyer's history) can link the two; timing and tenor class can hint
  6. 秘A buyer's invoices belong togetherHiddenEach invoice uses its own buyer key. A lender the buyer showed its history to can find the buyer's next invoice on the board
  7. 秘Advance and repayment amountsHiddenShielded; visible only to the parties and to whoever holds their viewing key

Try it

Read a board entry as an outsider

You will see the entry's kind, N, the financier commitment, the two classes and any history tag, or a settlement's tag and on-time flag. No party, amount or document. Checking a signature needs the supplier's key, which is only inside the invoice.

Runs entirely in your browser. No trackers, no third-party requests.
