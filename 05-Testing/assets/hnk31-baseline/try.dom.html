What the board does not stop

The board stops one signed invoice from being financed twice. It is not credit diligence.

  1. 第一

    A fake invoice that the buyer itself co-signs. The buyer's signature is real, so the board sees a new invoice.

  2. 第二

    One delivery split into two invoices that the buyer both signs.

  3. 第三

    A financier that does not check the board before paying.
