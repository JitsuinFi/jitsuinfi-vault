第一話

The invoice is born

A supplier ships the goods and invoices the buyer: 0.0004 ZEC, due in 60 days.

An illustrated town plays the life of one invoice: the supplier ships goods, the buyer seals the invoice, only its fingerprint N is posted to a public board while the invoice sinks into a shielded vault, a financier advances cash, a copy sent to a second financier is refused because the same N is already on the board, and the buyer pays on the due date.

指紋 · Chapter 2

One fingerprint per invoice.

N is a BLAKE2b-256 hash of the invoice’s exact bytes. The same invoice always gives the same N; change one zat and almost every digit changes. The board keeps N, never the invoice.

Try it: this runs jitsuin-wasm in your browser. Nothing is sent.

Three seals, then the money moves.

The assignment letter hands the invoice to the financier. The supplier seals it, then the buyer, then the financier, each over the seals before. Only a whole chain releases the advance: Sign-to-Pay.

Each press makes a real Ed25519 seal in your browser with test keys. Nothing is sent.

二重 · Chapter 4

Pressed twice, caught once.

A supplier who signs one invoice for two financiers leaves two signatures on one N. The second financier finds N on the board, refuses, and keeps both entries: a proof anyone can check, offline.

This flow runs itself in your browser with jitsuin-wasm and test keys; every station is a real call. Nothing is sent.

    履歴 · Chapter 5

    Paid, and remembered.

    Every invoice the buyer receives adds a certificate to its own chain, tied to one entry on the board. A new financier checks the chain offline and learns how the buyer paid, never whom it paid. Leave one invoice out and the chain no longer closes.

    Eight certificates, their board entries and the settlements are made in your browser with jitsuin-wasm and test keys. Nothing is sent.

    結び · Chapter 6

    On testnet, it already happened.

    One invoice, TES-INV-0003, lived the whole story on Zcash testnet: registered, advanced, repaid, settled, then offered to a second financier and caught. These are its five real transactions.

    1. 01Registered

      shielded · block 4,509,299 · fee 0.0001 TAZ

      Register entry: the invoice’s N, financier A’s salted commitment, the supplier’s signature.

    2. 02Advanced

      shielded · fee 0.0001 TAZ

      The advance to the supplier, released by the three seals: the financier’s receipt and seal ride in the memo.

    3. 03Repaid

      shielded · fee 0.0001 TAZ

      The buyer pays the financier by ZIP-321; its receipt and claim seal ride in the memo.

    4. 04Settled

      shielded · fee 0.0001 TAZ

      Settled entry, signed by buyer and financier, on time. Without N, its tag cannot be linked to the register entry.

    5. 05Caught

      shielded · block 4,509,521

      The same N, offered to financier B. It reached the board second, so it loses, and the two entries are the double-signature proof.

    An explorer sees five shielded transactions. Parties, amounts and memos stay encrypted; only the holder of a viewing key reads them. Copy any id to look it up on a testnet explorer.
